# How to Request Indexing in Google Search Console

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Access Google Search Console
1. Go to: **https://search.google.com/search-console**
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Select your property: `fxsentinel.io`

### Step 2: Use URL Inspection Tool
1. **At the top of any Search Console page**, you'll see a search bar
2. **Type or paste your URL**: `https://fxsentinel.io/`
3. **Press Enter** or click the search icon

### Step 3: Request Indexing
1. The URL Inspection tool will show you the current status
2. Look for a button that says **"Request Indexing"** or **"Requesting"**
3. Click **"Request Indexing"**
4. Google will process your request (usually takes a few seconds)

### Step 4: Verify Status
- You'll see: "Indexing requested" or "URL is on Google"
- Google will crawl your page within a few days
- Check back in 1-2 weeks to see if it's indexed

## Visual Guide
- **Location**: Top search bar on any Search Console page
- **Button**: Usually appears after you inspect a URL
- **Status**: Shows "Indexing requested" when successful

## Alternative Method
If you can't find the search bar:
1. Go to: **https://search.google.com/search-console**
2. Click **"URL Inspection"** in the left sidebar
3. Enter your URL in the search box
4. Click **"Request Indexing"**

## Notes
- You can request indexing for up to 10 URLs per day
- Google may take 1-2 weeks to process
- For faster results, also submit your sitemap (already done)
