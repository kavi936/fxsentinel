# MVP Budget-Friendly Implementation Plan

## Updated Monthly Recurring Costs (MRC) – Telegram News Harvesting

### **Core Monthly Costs**
- **TradingView Market Data**: $0 (Free forever, real-time forex data)
- **Domain**: $2.92 ($35/year)
- **Railway Hosting**: $5-10 (assume $8 for planning)
- **OpenAI API (AI Analysis)**: $10-25 (assume $15 for planning)
- **Telegram Bot API (News Harvesting)**: $0
- **Watcher Guru Channel**: $0

**Total MRC:** ~$26/month (rounded for planning)

---

## **Revenue Model & Pricing**
- **Product Price**: $24.99/month
- **Free Trial**: 5 days (no charge, no revenue in trial period)
- **Launch Timeline**: 1 month from now

---

## **Break-Even & Profit Analysis**

### **1. Launch Month Cost**
- **Total Cost for Month 1**: ~$26
- **No revenue during free trial for each user**

### **2. Break-Even Calculation**
- **Break-even users needed:**
  - $26 (MRC) / $24.99 (per user) ≈ **1.04 users**
  - **You need 2 paying users to break even** (since you can't have a fraction of a user)

### **3. Realistic Revenue Goals**
- **Goal 1: Break Even**
  - **2 paying users** after their 5-day trial = $49.98 revenue, $23.98 profit
- **Goal 2: Modest Profit**
  - **5 paying users** = $124.95 revenue, $98.95 profit
- **Goal 3: Early Traction**
  - **10 paying users** = $249.90 revenue, $223.90 profit

### **4. User Acquisition Targets**
- **Assume 10% conversion from free trial to paid**
- To get 2 paid users, you need ~20 signups in month 1
- To get 10 paid users, you need ~100 signups in month 1

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**
- **Focus on acquiring at least 30-100 trial signups in the first month**
- **Monitor conversion rate** from trial to paid closely
- **Keep costs low by using Telegram for news harvesting**
- **Scale OpenAI usage as user base grows**
- **Reinvest early profits into marketing and product improvements**
