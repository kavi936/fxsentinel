FXSentinel – AI Macro Intelligence for Forex Traders

FXSentinel is a market intelligence platform for forex traders that turns global macro news into clear, actionable market insight. The app provides a real-time dashboard with an economic calendar, AI-powered news analysis, macro impact on major FX pairs and gold, prediction-style markets that show how the crowd is pricing future scenarios, and instant Telegram alerts so traders never miss market-moving events.

The FXSentinel landing page explains how the platform helps both beginner and professional traders understand the "why" behind FX moves, with sections covering live macro alerts, news-driven analysis, AI explanations of each release, prediction-style probability charts, pricing options, FAQs, and social proof from traders. The dashboard focuses on fundamentals rather than price-only technical signals, helping users build macro intuition and prepare for central bank decisions, CPI, NFP, GDP and other high-impact economic releases.

FXSentinel also includes a blog with educational articles and market analysis on topics like AI-powered news analysis, central bank decisions, forex market sentiment, turning news into actionable signals, decoding volatility, geopolitical risk in 2025, Fed versus ECB divergence, gold as a safe haven and inflation hedge, and how institutional adoption of Bitcoin and other macro themes affect currency markets and XAU/USD. Each blog post is written for retail traders who want a plain-language explanation of how macro events translate into trading opportunities.

The site makes clear that FXSentinel is not a broker, not a copy-trading or signals provider, and does not guarantee trading profits. It provides tools, alerts and analysis to support independent decision making, with transparent pricing, a free plan for getting started, and a paid premium plan for higher limits and more advanced features.
