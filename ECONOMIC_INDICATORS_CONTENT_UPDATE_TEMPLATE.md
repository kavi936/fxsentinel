## 📝 Content Sections Per Indicator

Each indicator has **6 content sections** that need updating:

1. **Key Insights** (4 bullet points) - Current market analysis, policy context, and forward-looking implications
2. **Overview** - What the indicator is, why it matters to traders
3. **Measurement** - How it's calculated, who publishes it, release frequency
4. **Impact** - Currency and market impact (FX, rates, equities, commodities)
5. **Context** - Current policy context, recent trends, institutional views
6. **Trader Implications** - What traders should monitor, thresholds, FXSentinel usage

## ✍️ Writing Guidelines

### Key Insights Format
- Use 4 bullet points
- Focus on current policy stance, market expectations, key drivers, and trading implications
- Keep each bullet point concise but informative
- Include specific numbers and dates where relevant

### Context Section (Most Critical)
- Always include current reading and date
- Describe recent trend (rising/falling/stable)
- Include recent policy decisions with dates
- Add institutional bank commentary and forecasts
- Mention economic backdrop (inflation, growth, labor market)
- Include political/structural factors if relevant

### Highlighting System
- Use `[[double brackets]]` around key terms and concepts
- These will be rendered with red underlines in the UI
- Highlight: policy terms, key numbers, market concepts, trading implications

### Tone & Style
- Professional but accessible
- Trader-focused language
- Forward-looking perspective
- Data-driven analysis
- Avoid jargon without explanation

## 💡 Tips for Effective Updates

1. **Start with Key Insights** - These are the most visible and should reflect current conditions
2. **Focus on Context** - This is where most of the "fresh" content goes
3. **Cross-reference** - Ensure consistency across related indicators (e.g., inflation and interest rates)
4. **Use Specific Numbers** - "3.2%" is better than "around 3%"
5. **Include Dates** - "November 2025" is better than "recently"
6. **Cite Sources** - Reference specific bank reports or central bank statements
7. **Update Trader Implications** - Ensure thresholds and monitoring points are current
8. **Check Highlighting** - Ensure important terms are properly highlighted with `[[brackets]]`
